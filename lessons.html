Our Instructors

Hunter Ward

Pitching

Hunter Ward is a former collegiate pitcher with three years of experience at UMass Boston, where he developed as a competitive pitcher at the NCAA level. During his collegiate career, Hunter also played summer baseball for the Nashua Silver Knights of the Futures Collegiate Baseball League, earning Futures League All-Star honors. Hunter is passionate about helping players improve through personalized instruction focused on pitching mechanics, velocity development, command, and overall baseball IQ. Drawing from his collegiate and summer league experience, he works to create a positive, challenging environment that helps players build confidence and reach their full potential.

Jeffrey Lugo

Catching

Jeffrey Lugo is a former three-year varsity catcher at Nashua High School South and was widely regarded as one of the top defensive catchers in New Hampshire. For the past three years, he has worked with catchers of all ages to develop the skills needed to succeed behind the plate. His instruction focuses on receiving and framing, blocking, and pitch selection, helping catchers improve their defensive consistency, game awareness, and leadership on the field.

Conner Comeau

Strength & Conditioning

Conner is an ISSA Certified Personal Trainer specializing in strength and conditioning, athletic performance, nutrition, and weight management. With a competitive background in football, baseball, wrestling, and boxing, he brings a well-rounded approach to athletic development. At MVP Training Facility, Conner works with athletes of all sports and ages: younger athletes focus on movement, coordination, and foundational strength, while high school athletes and older players use individualized strength programs designed to improve speed, power, and overall athletic performance.

Dakota Bilodeau

Infield

Dakota Bilodeau was a four-year Division I infielder at Bryant University, where she made over 90 appearances and started more than 60 games in the infield. She recorded 25 stolen bases and led the team in stolen bases in 2020 and 2021. As a senior in 2021, she started 18 games at second base, led her team in walks and stolen bases, and earned NEC Academic Honor Roll recognition. During her career, Dakota hit five home runs and consistently impacted the game with her defensive reliability. She brings Division I experience and strong infield fundamentals to developing players.

Noah Wachter

Pitching

Noah Wachter is a seasoned collegiate pitcher who competes at Southern New Hampshire University after transferring from Plymouth State University, where he ranked 6th all-time in single-season strikeouts. At SNHU, Noah is playing his final season in 2026 and hopes to remain a significant factor in the team's success. Noah brings a competitive mindset and a strong analytical mind for baseball, and loves incorporating Rapsodo analysis into his lessons.

Cam Cook

Hitting, Infield & College Recruiting

Cam Cook is currently the General Manager of the Nashua Silver Knights in the Futures League. He is entering his fifth season at the helm, in which he has won two league championships and seen five of his players selected in the MLB Draft. Before entering the front office, Cam was the Silver Knights third baseman from 2016 to 2017, winning the championship both years, and set the single season hits record while capturing the 2017 FCBL batting title. Cam aims to help develop hitters and infielders throughout the offseason, while assisting high school level players in the college recruiting process.

  • 4x Futures League Champion
  • 2020 Futures League Executive of the Year
  • 2017 Futures League Batting Champion
  • Futures League Single Season Hits Record (81)
  • 2x All New England 2B
  • 3x All Commonwealth Coast Conference 2B
  • Nichols College Single Season Hits Record (67)
  • Nichols College Career Hits Record (210)

Brendan Martin "BMart"

Hitting & Infield

Brendan is currently the hitting and infield coach for Nashua South. He also assists MLB scouts at Area Code games and showcases throughout the summer, and runs the Futures League's Pro Day. Brendan played at Nichols College, where he was an All-Conference shortstop his sophomore season, starting 71 of 73 career games and posting a career batting average of .312. A Nashua native, he played high school baseball for Nashua North, where he was also the starting quarterback. Brendan's expertise is in hitting and infield defense.

Awards

  • 2016 All Commonwealth Coast Conference SS
  • 2016 All-Academic Commonwealth Coast Conference
