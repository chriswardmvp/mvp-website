Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

MV Prospects Baseball / Merrimack Valley Prospects LLC ("MVP Baseball," "we," "us") operates this website. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

1. Who We Are

Merrimack Valley Prospects LLC, 472 Amherst Street, Nashua, NH 03063. Contact: info@mvpbaseballnh.com. This website is built and maintained by Baseline Studio (baselinestudio.org).

2. Information We Collect

This site itself does not collect personal information directly. Contact, tryout, and private lesson inquiries are handled through Byga, our registration platform; information you submit through those forms is governed by Byga's own privacy policy. This site uses cookie-free analytics (GoatCounter) to see general traffic patterns, such as which pages are visited; this does not collect any personally identifiable information, does not use cookies, and does not track you across other websites.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through contact forms is used only to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our programs serve players under the age of 13. All contact forms on this site are intended to be completed by a parent or guardian on behalf of a child, not by the child directly. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information without parental consent, contact us at info@mvpbaseballnh.com and we will remove it promptly.

5. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry. You may request deletion at any time by emailing us.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email info@mvpbaseballnh.com to exercise any of these rights.

7. Third-Party Services

This site embeds a Facebook Page Plugin to display our public Facebook feed. When you view this embed, Facebook may set cookies and collect data per their own policy at facebook.com/privacy/policy. Contact and registration forms link out to Byga, and lesson bookings link out to Swift (RunSwift); information submitted there is governed by those platforms' own policies. This site uses GoatCounter for cookie-free, privacy-friendly analytics; see goatcounter.com/privacy. External links (Perfect Game) are governed by that platform's own policy.

8. Cookies

This site itself does not set cookies. The Facebook embed may set third-party cookies. You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings.

9. Accessibility

We want this site to be usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, or other assistive technology. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. Features of this site include:

  • → A "Skip to main content" link and page landmarks for screen reader and keyboard navigation.
  • → Keyboard-accessible menus, forms, and buttons, with visible focus indicators.
  • → Form fields with associated labels, required-field indicators, and error/success messages announced to assistive technology.
  • → Descriptive alternative text on informational images; purely decorative images and icons are hidden from screen readers.
  • → Text and background color combinations that meet WCAG AA contrast minimums.
  • → Reduced motion for visitors who have that preference set in their operating system or browser.

Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and some parts of the site may not yet be fully accessible, including content from third-party embeds (such as the Facebook feed) and external booking platforms (Perfect Game, RunSwift/Swift) that we do not control. If you encounter a barrier using this site, or need information in an alternate format, please contact us at info@mvpbaseballnh.com. Please describe the issue and the page where you encountered it, and we will work to address it as quickly as possible.
