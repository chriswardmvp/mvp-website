About MV Prospects

Ten-plus years of travel baseball in southern New Hampshire and northern Massachusetts.

Our Story

The MVProspects started in 2014 as the Greater Nashua Prospects, an AAU club founded by Steve Adams, Chris Ward, and Lance Hedquist. From the start, the mission was clear: provide affordable, quality travel baseball and help players develop their skills and their love for the game.

In 2020, the organization became the Merrimack Valley Prospects LLC, expanded beyond Nashua, and joined the NH Select League. By 2024, the program rebranded as MVP Baseball, with travel teams called the MV Prospects and a training facility that opened in 2025.

Over the years MVP has grown to 16 teams, sent 13 groups and 156 players to Cooperstown, and created memories for hundreds of kids across the region. Success here isn't only measured in wins. It's about a love for the game, building community, and helping every player grow on and off the field.

Our Timeline

2020

Merrimack Valley Prospects LLC

The organization expands beyond Nashua, joins the NH Select League, and incorporates as an LLC to support a growing program.

2024

Rebrand to MVP Baseball

New name, new logo, expanded reach. Travel teams remain the MV Prospects. 16 active teams across all age groups.

2025

MVP Training Facility Opens

The organization opens its own facility at 472 Amherst Street in Nashua with turf cages, pitching mounds, and year-round availability.
