Competitive Travel Baseball for Southern NH

MV Prospects fields 16 teams, 9U through 17U, competing in the Perfect Game League across New England. Since 2014, the goal has been the same: quality coaching and real competition without the sky-high price tag.

Along the way we've sent 13 teams to Cooperstown Dreams Park, opened our own training facility, and helped hundreds of players develop on and off the field. The name has changed since our Greater Nashua Prospects days. The mission hasn't.
