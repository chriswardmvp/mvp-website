Facility Overview

MVP Training Facility is our six-tunnel indoor training space at 472 Amherst Street in Nashua. Four full-length tunnels support hitting, mound-equipped pitching, and full batting practice, and two shorter tunnels are built for tee work, soft toss, and front toss. Every tunnel includes pitching machine access. It's home to MV Prospects private lessons and Training Clubs, and it's also open to active members for individual tunnel time and to outside teams for practice rentals.

All memberships run through July 31 to align with the season and do not auto-renew. You'll get a renewal notice and choose whether to come back next season. Sessions are 30 minutes, but you can combine two of them for a 1-hour rental.

Tunnel Rental Details

All six tunnels include pitching machine access, with mound-equipped work in the four full-length tunnels.

Individual Tunnel Rental

▼

Individual Tunnel Rental lets active members book tunnel time for personal practice, alone, with a parent or coach, or with a small training group. Available across all six tunnels, with pitching machines and mound-equipped tunnels included.

How it works:

  • Active membership required: Bronze, Silver, or Gold members can book using included monthly sessions or pay the member overage rate for additional time
  • All tunnels priced the same: book any of the four full-length tunnels or the two short tunnels (A & B) at the same rate
  • PIN-code self-access: your booking confirmation includes a PIN that grants entry during your reserved time
  • Family-shared booking: your membership and bookings cover the whole family

About the tunnels:

  • Four full-length tunnels support hitting, pitching off the mound, full BP, and group practice
  • Two short tunnels (A & B) are sized for hitting work: soft toss, tee work, coach pitch, and front toss. Pitching machines and mound work aren't available in A & B

Pitching machines: We currently have 3 baseball pitching machines and 1 softball pitching machine, available first-come, first-served during your reserved tunnel time. Machines can be moved between full-length tunnels, not into A & B. Machines require operator knowledge, there's no on-site staff to train you on setup, ball loading, or speed and location settings. If you're not familiar with operating them, plan your session around tee work, soft toss, front toss, or coach and parent pitch.

Dual Tunnel Rental

▼

Dual Tunnel Rental gives teams two connecting full-length tunnels for structured practice: enough space for split work (hitting in one, pitching in the other), parallel stations, or full team batting practice with multiple cages running.

What's included:

  • Two connecting full-length tunnels with full hitting, pitching, and BP capability
  • Mound-equipped pitching tunnels for live bullpens
  • PIN-code self-access during your reserved practice window
  • Pitching machines available first-come, first-served (3 baseball, 1 softball)

Booking: Pre-approved teams only. Your team's Swift account is set up with booking permissions and a rate based on your team type (school, rec, travel, etc). Pricing varies by team category and shows on your account. New teams should contact us to discuss availability, pricing, and account setup.

Full Facility Rental gives teams exclusive access to all four full-length tunnels for full team practices, scrimmages, tryouts, or training events.
