Facility Overview

MVP Training Facility is southern New Hampshire's indoor home for serious baseball and softball training, and the home of the MV Prospects. Located in Nashua's Amherst Street retail corridor, our facility is built around giving players the space, equipment, and instruction to put in real work, whether they're preparing for a season, fixing something specific, or just keeping their game sharp.

Individual Tunnel Rental

Book tunnel time for personal practice, alone, with a parent or coach, or with a small training group.

How it works:

  • Active membership required: Bronze, Silver, or Gold members can book using included monthly sessions or pay the member overage rate for additional time
  • All tunnels priced the same: book any of the four full-length tunnels or the two short tunnels (A & B) at the same rate
  • PIN-code self-access: your booking confirmation includes a PIN that grants entry during your reserved time
  • Family-shared booking: your membership and bookings cover the whole family

About the tunnels:

  • Four full-length tunnels support hitting, pitching off the mound, full BP, and group practice
  • Two short tunnels (A & B) are sized for hitting work: soft toss, tee work, coach pitch, and front toss. Pitching machines and mound work aren't available in A & B

Pitching machines: We currently have 3 baseball pitching machines and 1 softball pitching machine, available first-come, first-served during your reserved tunnel time. Machines can be moved between full-length tunnels, not into A & B. There's no on-site staff to train you on setup, so plan your session around tee work, soft toss, front toss, or coach and parent pitch if you're not familiar with operating them.

Dual Tunnel Rental

Two connecting full-length tunnels for team practice: split work, parallel stations, or full team BP with multiple cages running.

What's included:

  • Two connecting full-length tunnels with full hitting, pitching, and BP capability
  • Mound-equipped pitching tunnels for live bullpens
  • PIN-code self-access during your reserved practice window
  • Pitching machines available first-come, first-served (3 baseball, 1 softball)

Booking: Pre-approved teams only. Your team's Swift account is set up with booking permissions and a rate based on your team type (school, rec, travel, etc). Pricing varies by team category and shows on your account.

New teams must email info@MVPTrainingFacility.com to discuss availability, pricing, and account setup.

Full Facility Rental

Exclusive access to all four full-length tunnels for full team practices, scrimmages, tryouts, or training events.

What's included:

  • All four full-length tunnels with full hitting, pitching, and BP capability
  • Mound-equipped pitching tunnels for live bullpens and pitching staff work
  • PIN-code self-access during your reserved window
  • Pitching machines available first-come, first-served (3 baseball, 1 softball)

Important: Tunnels A & B are not included. They stay available for individual member rentals during your team's session, even when the rest of the facility is yours. Contact us in advance if you need A & B access during your event.

Booking: Pre-approved teams only. Your team's Swift account is set up with booking permissions and a rate based on your team type. Pricing varies by team category and shows on your account.

MVP is a real training facility, not a coin-op cage. There's no on-site staff during tunnel sessions, and outside coaches can't be brought in for individual sessions (a facility insurance requirement), so someone in your group should be comfortable running BP, tee work, or a pitching machine. Need more instruction? Our Lessons and Training Clubs are built for exactly that.

Team practices and events should be run by coaches who know how to manage the space. Delayed replay video and Rapsodo technology are available for any team to use during their session.
