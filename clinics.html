Clinics & Drop-In Clubs for MV Prospects Players

As an MV Prospects player, you have access to all clinics and drop-in clubs at the MVP Training Facility, with discounted pricing through your included Bronze membership.

Drop-In Clubs

Weekly group training led by our experienced instructors. Pay as you go, register session by session. No multi-week commitments to work around. Show up when it fits your schedule.

  • →
    Hitting Club: Swing mechanics, approach, and game-speed reps
  • →
    Pitching Club: Mechanics, command, and pitch design
  • →
    Catching Club: Receiving, blocking, framing, footwork, and game management
  • →
    Strength & Conditioning Club: Baseball-specific strength, mobility, and athletic development

MV Prospects players get a Bronze member discount on every session.

Specialty Clinics

Throughout the year we run specialty clinics on specific skills, age groups, or focused training topics. Schedules vary; check Swift for what's currently on the calendar.
