Training Club Details

Hitting Club

Cap: 8 Players

Hitting Club is small-group hitting training designed to give players consistent, structured reps under the eye of an experienced instructor, at a fraction of the cost of private lessons.

What to expect in each session:

  • Structured warm-up and tee work to lock in mechanics
  • Front toss and live BP with instructor feedback on every round
  • Delayed instant replay review: watch your swing on screen seconds after each rep
  • Rapsodo for in-session feedback on closing rounds: see exit velocity and launch angle on screen to make in-the-moment adjustments
  • Take-home reps: no waiting in long lines, small group sizes mean every player gets real work

Sessions are split by age group for age-appropriate training. Cap is 8 players per session to keep the work intense and the feedback personal.

About Rapsodo data: Hitting Club uses Rapsodo for live, on-screen feedback during the session, real numbers driving real adjustments as you work. For hitters who want their data tracked over time with full Rapsodo portal access, performance history, and trend analysis, see our Rapsodo Data Day specialty clinic or book a private lesson with a Rapsodo-equipped instructor.

Pricing: Pay per session, or save with a 10-pack punch card that works across all Training Clubs. Active members get an additional discount on every session, see below for details.

Pitching Club

Cap: 6 Players

Pitching Club is focused, structured group training for pitchers serious about getting better. Mound-equipped tunnels, Rapsodo Pitching technology, and experienced instructors in a small-group format that prioritizes arm health and real development.

What to expect each session:

  • Dynamic warm-up and arm-care routine built around long-term arm health
  • Bullpen work in mound-equipped tunnels with instructor feedback on mechanics, sequencing, and pitch shape
  • Rapsodo Pitching for in-session feedback: see velocity, spin, and pitch shape on screen right after each pitch to make in-the-moment adjustments
  • Small group format: no more than 6 pitchers per session means every arm gets real attention

Sessions are split by age group for age-appropriate training and arm-load management. Sessions run 60 minutes, shorter than Hitting Club because pitching work is intense and arm health matters. Book ahead; pitching mound availability is limited.

About Rapsodo data: Pitching Club uses Rapsodo for live, on-screen feedback during the session, real numbers driving real adjustments while you're working. For pitchers who want their data tracked over time with full Rapsodo portal access, performance history, and trend analysis, see our Rapsodo Data Day specialty clinic or book a private lesson with a Rapsodo-equipped instructor.

Strength & Conditioning Club

Cap: 8 Players

Strength & Conditioning Club is athletic development designed specifically for baseball and softball players, the speed, agility, mobility, and durability work that makes everything else on the field better. No heavy weights, no gym intimidation, just the kind of athletic foundation that lets players hit harder, throw harder, and stay healthier across a long season.

What to expect each session:

  • Dynamic warm-up and movement prep: proper warm-up routines players can use before games and practices
  • Speed and agility work: first-step quickness, change of direction, sprint mechanics
  • Band-based strength training targeting the muscles baseball and softball players actually use, with full progression options for younger and older athletes
  • Core and stability training, the foundation for transferring power from the ground through the swing or throw
  • Conditioning circuits: sport-specific endurance work that translates to late-game performance
  • Mobility and recovery work that prevents injury and extends careers

Sessions are split by age group for age-appropriate progressions. Capped at 8 players per session to ensure proper form and individual feedback.

Why no weights: Younger and developing athletes get more performance gains from mastering bodyweight movement, building athletic foundations, and using bands for resistance than from loading heavy weights too early. Our program is designed by people who understand baseball-specific athletic development, including power production, rotational strength, arm-care support, and lower-body mechanics, and delivered with tools and techniques that work for players from youth through high school.
